# Player placeholder sprites

These four sheets are unlicensed temporary development placeholders supplied locally by the team.

- They may be committed only while the repository remains private.
- They must not be included in a public build or public repository.
- Replace them with team-authored or properly licensed 32×32 player art before release.
- Runtime uses the down, up, and side sheets; the left sheet is archived but intentionally unused because left-facing art is mirrored from the side sheet.

The sheets use 20×30 frames separated by one transparent pixel. They intentionally deviate from the final 32×32 asset baseline.
