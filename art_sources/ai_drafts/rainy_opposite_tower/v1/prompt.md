## 生成提示词

Create one ultra-wide 3:1 panoramic environment background plate for a low-resolution 2D cyberpunk action game. View from inside a high-rise, looking almost straight across at a massive neighboring skyscraper at rainy night. Near-frontal elevation with only a very subtle upward perspective; verticals and horizontals remain highly usable for overlay alignment. The opposing building fills the full image height and is cropped beyond the top and bottom, creating close-range megastructure scale. Keep left and right edges crop-safe and extendable.

IMPORTANT GAMEPLAY VALUE STRUCTURE: the exterior skyscraper is the brighter plane, while the unseen battle-floor foreground will be darker. Use luminous cool gray-blue glass, broad soft fog glow, and restrained interior office illumination. Keep the building's local contrast, saturation, and detail sharpness subdued so player silhouettes and future ads remain readable. Sparse warm amber windows and tiny dim red aviation/service lights provide accents.

The six screens must be clearly separated by load-bearing columns, equipment strips, and maintenance gaps, not a neat webpage grid. Screens are powered-off near-black blue display surfaces with subtle standby edge glow and metal bezels. No content inside them.

Very little sky. Subtle wetness, condensation glow, damp reflections, and atmospheric haze, but NO visible rain streaks or individual raindrops baked into the image. This is the outside-city plate only: do not add the viewer-side glass overlay, interior reflections, room furniture, battle floor, characters, or interface.

STYLE: handcrafted low-resolution game environment concept art, pixel-compatible shapes, crisp large architectural masses with selectively softened atmospheric layers, restrained cinematic cyberpunk, deep blue-black and cool gray-blue palette. Avoid photorealistic camera look and avoid excessively fine micro-detail. Designed to be downsampled cleanly into a 640x360 game.

ABSOLUTELY NO readable text, letters, numbers, logos, brands, people, close vehicles, advertisements, posters, or recognizable IP. No 16:9 framing; target an unmistakably ultra-wide 3:1 panorama.
